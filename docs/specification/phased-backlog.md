# Evidence-gated product backlog

Priority is expressed through dependencies and exit evidence. Dates are planning aids and cannot override a failed gate.

## Phase 1 — Trusted career loop

### P1.1 Professional identity

- Complete structured education, experience, speciality, species, language, and visibility behavior.
- Validate progressive onboarding and profile completion with real professionals.
- Maintain English/Arabic-ready and RTL-safe schemas and interfaces.

### P1.2 Trust and verification

- Complete reviewer operations, information requests, verification decisions, expiry, and revocation.
- Track verification turnaround, manual effort, cost, and decision quality.
- Preserve evidence provenance and private-file access controls.

### P1.3 Recruitment completion

- Normalize job requirements and professional preferences.
- Add explainable matching and employer filters.
- Complete application, interview, offer, employment, and portfolio propagation journeys.
- Produce outcome-based recruitment reporting without fabricated metrics.

### Phase 1 exit gate

- Real professional profiles and evidence submissions.
- Real reviewer decisions with measured turnaround and cost.
- Verified employers, live vacancies, and candidate actions.
- Interviews, offers, and confirmed employment.
- Evidence of employer willingness to pay.

## Phase 2 — Licensing and learning

### P2.0 Foundation

- Extend country/taxonomy ownership for authorities, licence types, requirement types, learning types, and CPD units.
- Define provider permissions and course-publication governance.
- Add contact verification/MFA foundations required for higher-trust operations.

### P2.1 Authority and pathway administration

- Curator creates jurisdiction and authority records.
- Curator creates a draft pathway version with sourced requirements.
- Reviewer approves and publishes a pathway version.
- Superseded pathways remain queryable and active enrollments retain their original version.

### P2.2 Professional licensing workspace

- Professional selects jurisdiction and target licence.
- System evaluates explainable eligibility.
- Professional enrolls in a pathway and receives a checklist.
- Verified evidence is reused; missing items are clearly identified.
- Professional records external submission reference and status.
- Deadlines, expiry, and renewals produce notifications.
- Issued licences enter the credential wallet.

### P2.3 Provider workspace

- Verified education organization enables provider capability.
- Provider managers create courses and webinars.
- Content passes publication review.
- Provider manages sessions, instructors, learners, attendance, and results within organization scope.

### P2.4 Professional learning workspace

- Professional discovers and filters free/institution-sponsored learning.
- Professional enrolls and resumes progress.
- Live and recorded delivery states are clear.
- Attendance and assessment rules are enforced.
- Completion produces a verifiable certificate and CPD record.
- Certificate/CPD updates professional evidence and licensing readiness.

### P2.5 Library and events pilot

- Curated books, notes, question-bank items, cases, surgery records, and imaging references use a common content-governance model.
- Events/workshops distinguish educational content from labelled sponsorship.
- Clinical contributions require authorship, review status, privacy, and de-identification.

### Phase 2 exit gate

- One country pathway is operationally validated by qualified domain owners.
- Professionals complete real checklists and reuse verified evidence.
- At least one verified institution publishes and runs real learning.
- Attendance/assessment produces issuer-verifiable certificates.
- Certificates update CPD, portfolio evidence, and pathway readiness.
- Support effort, content quality, and learner activation meet approved thresholds.

## Phase 3 — Facility foundation

### P3.1 Facility topology

- Organization creates branches/facilities and departments.
- Membership access is scoped to facility and department.
- Staff access ends when employment or membership ends.

### P3.2 Owner and animal identity

- Account adds an owner profile without creating a second login.
- Owner records animals and relationship history.
- Facility performs governed duplicate detection and identity reconciliation.

### P3.3 Consent and appointments

- Consent records purpose, scope, beneficiary, expiry, withdrawal, and audit.
- Appointment lifecycle covers request, confirmation, arrival, cancellation, no-show, and completion.

### P3.4 Minimal encounter

- Encounter binds animal, owner authorization, facility, practitioner, role, timing, reason, and outcome.
- Completed encounter emits portfolio and analytics-ready events without exposing identifiable clinical data.

### Phase 3 exit gate

- Pilot facilities safely manage branches, staff, owners, animals, consent, appointments, and encounters.
- Permission, duplicate-identity, support, and data-quality targets are met.

## Phase 4 — Clinical operations

### P4.1 Clinical record foundation

- History, observations, physical examination, problem/diagnosis, procedure, prescription, and follow-up.
- Amendments preserve original author, source, reason, and time.

### P4.2 Diagnostics

- Laboratory orders, specimens, results, and interpretation.
- Imaging orders, studies, media references, reports, and interpretation.
- Integration adapters preserve external identifiers and provenance.

### P4.3 Surgery and critical care

- Surgical episode, participants, checklist, anaesthesia, procedures, outcome, and complications.
- Emergency/critical-care acuity, timed observations, orders, handoffs, and outcome.

### P4.4 Medication and stock linkage

- Prescription and dispensing remain distinct actions.
- Clinical consumption updates inventory through auditable transactions.

### Phase 4 exit gate

- Clinical governance approves workflows and terminology.
- Pilot records satisfy safety, completeness, correction, privacy, and audit requirements.
- Support and migration processes are operational.

## Phase 5 — Commerce and intelligence

### P5.1 Entitlements and subscriptions

- Define entitlements from validated value, not arbitrary feature withholding.
- Add invoices, refunds, taxes, and support only with approved payment partners.

### P5.2 Partner commerce

- Govern supplier identity, catalogue, order, fulfilment, dispute, commission, and payout boundaries.

### P5.3 Analytics and benchmarking

- Metrics have definitions, data-quality thresholds, cohorts, and permitted users.
- Benchmarks require sufficient sample size and comparability.

### P5.4 Epidemiology and AI

- De-identification, consent, lawful purpose, quality, and clinical oversight are prerequisites.
- AI outputs show source context, limitations, confidence, and required professional review.

### Phase 5 exit gate

- Transaction, data-density, governance, and customer-value thresholds are achieved.
- Independent security, privacy, and clinical-governance reviews approve release.

## Backlog admission rule

An item enters an implementation plan only when it has an owner, persona, outcome, dependency, permission, state model, data classification, acceptance criteria, and verification method. A node from the source PDF is not sufficient on its own.
