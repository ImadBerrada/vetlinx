# VetLinX MVP scope

## Objective

Prove the first trusted career loop:

1. A veterinarian creates a structured professional identity.
2. The veterinarian submits credentials and licences as evidence.
3. VetLinX reviews and records verification decisions.
4. A verified organization publishes a structured vacancy.
5. The veterinarian applies, interviews, and accepts an offer.
6. The organization confirms employment.
7. Confirmed employment strengthens the veterinarian's portfolio.

## Included

- Account registration, authentication, recovery, and session management.
- Veterinarian profile, education, experience, specialties, species, and languages.
- Credentials wallet, licences, secure evidence files, and verification history.
- Reviewer queues, decisions, requests for information, expiry, and revocation.
- Public portfolio, privacy controls, ATS CV, and PDF export.
- Organization onboarding, verification, memberships, and recruiter roles.
- Jobs, applications, interviews, offers, employment, and status histories.
- Notifications, taxonomy, country configuration, audit, and operational health.
- English and Arabic-ready interfaces, including RTL foundations.

## Explicitly excluded from MVP

- Facility practice-management workflows.
- Animal and owner medical records.
- Prescribing, pharmacy, inventory, laboratory, and imaging workflows.
- Telemedicine and paid consultations.
- Courses, CPD marketplace, insurance, procurement, and research marketplaces.
- Epidemiology, enterprise intelligence, and clinical AI.

These exclusions are architectural modules for later phases, not navigation items or partially implemented features in the MVP.

The approved portfolio sequence and next-phase scope are defined in:

- [Evidence-gated product backlog](specification/phased-backlog.md)
- [Phase 2 licensing and learning specification](specification/phase-2-licensing-learning.md)

Phase 2 planning does not expand the Phase 1 release boundary. It starts only after the pilot exit evidence below is accepted.

## Pilot exit evidence

- Veterinarians complete structured profiles and submit credentials.
- Reviewers complete real verification cases with acceptable turnaround and cost.
- Verified employers publish real vacancies and act on candidates.
- Applications progress to interviews, offers, and confirmed employment.
- Employers demonstrate willingness to pay for trusted recruitment outcomes.
